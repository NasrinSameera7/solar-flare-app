Solar Flare Classification

B & C Class

Weakest solar flares. C-class may cause brief radio blackouts on the sunlit side of Earth. Generally harmless to satellites and power grids.

Low Risk
M Class

Medium flares. Can cause brief radio blackouts and minor radiation storms. May trigger geomagnetic disturbances if associated with a fast CME.

Moderate Risk
X Class

Most powerful flares. Can cause wide-area radio blackouts, severe radiation storms, and major geomagnetic storms capable of disrupting power grids.

Severe Risk
